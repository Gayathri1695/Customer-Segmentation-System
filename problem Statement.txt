TOPIC  --  Customer Segmentation System

1. Problem Statement:

                  Businesses have many customers, but not all customers behave in the same way.

For example:

Some customers buy frequently.
Some spend a lot of money.
Some purchase only occasionally.
Some customers may have stopped purchasing.
  
      If the company treats all customers equally, it may miss opportunities for personalized marketing and better customer retention.

The problem is to develop an AIML-based system that analyzes customer-related data and groups customers into meaningful segments based on their similarities and purchasing behavior.


2. Identify AIML:

1. Domain: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML).
2. Machine Learning Type: Unsupervised Learning.
3. Problem Type: Clustering.
4. Algorithm: K-Means Clustering.
5. Target Variable: No predefined target variable, as the system automatically identifies customer segments.


Customer → Premium
Customer → Regular
Customer → Inactive


3. Define Objective:

The main objective of this project is to develop a Machine Learning model that segments customers into different groups based on their personal characteristics, purchasing behavior, and spending patterns.


1. To collect and preprocess customer-related data.
2. To identify important factors that describe customer behavior.
3. To analyze customer purchasing and spending patterns.
4. To group customers into meaningful segments using clustering techniques.
5. To train and apply different clustering algorithms.
6. To evaluate and compare the performance of the clustering models.
7. To identify high-value, regular, and low-value customer groups.
8. To use the identified customer segments for targeted marketing and better business decision-making.
